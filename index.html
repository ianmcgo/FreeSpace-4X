AI Opponents

Add or remove AI opponents and set each one's empire name, race and intelligence level. Every opponent starts with a pre-generated empire name you can change. Choose Random for a race or intelligence level and the game picks one when the galaxy is created.

Technology Research Matrix & Tech Tree

Research is paid in Resources. The share of your annual Resources set on the Economy screen flows into the field chosen here. When a field collects enough Resources it advances one level, and the next level costs more.

Empire Economy & Budget Allocation

Resources are your industrial labor: they come from colonists and operating factories. Ore (Ironium, Boranium, Germanium) is the raw matter dug up by mines. Everything you build needs both. Split your Resources between research and planetary manufacturing; unspent manufacturing Resources are lost. Total must equal 100%.

⚙ Advanced Options

Tick any option to add it to this game. These settings are locked in when you press Create Galaxy and are saved with the game.

🏆 Victory Conditions

Tick the conditions that can win the game and set their values. Owning 60% of all planets is ticked to start with. With nothing ticked the game never ends on its own. Every empire, AI included, is judged by the same rules.
Own at least % of all planets (10 to 100)
Reach level (1 to 26) in of the 6 fields (1 to 6)
Score of at least (1,000 to 1,000,000)
Have a score at least % higher than the second place empire (1 to 1,000)
Own at least capital ships (2 to 1,000)
Have the highest score after years (10 to 1,000,000)
A winner must meet of the above criteria to win
At least years must pass before a winner is declared (10 to 1,000,000)

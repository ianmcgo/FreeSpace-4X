FreeSpace 4x Version 0.7.26 Ultimate Guide

  • Configurable Victory Conditions: Customize win parameters (Domination, Score, Tech Supremacy, Elimination) from the New Game Setup menu.
  • Tactical Star Map Zoom & System Spacing: Zooming in on the Tactical Star Map gently separates system coordinates so dense clusters are much easier to click and select.
  • Habitability Curves: Planets near your target Gravity, Temperature, and Radiation yield higher habitability percentages and faster population growth.
  • Automated Waypoint Orders: Assign specific arrival tasks like colonizing, laying minefields, or sweeping enemy mines upon fleet waypoint completion.
  • Mass Drivers & Packets: Launch ballistic mineral packets across the stars to fuel remote planetary expansion without transport fleets.

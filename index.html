FreeSpace 4x Version 0.7.35 Ultimate Guide

  • Non-Overlapping Star Map: Star systems are procedurally generated with guaranteed minimum separation spacing so they never overlap even when fully zoomed in.
  • Direct Fleet Movement: Click any system on the tactical map to view distance and turn travel time, then click Move Fleet Here to dispatch your fleet! Monitor ship status via the Fleet Movement button.
  • Turn-Based Scouting & Buoys: Send scout ships or deploy Remote Scanner Buoys to map systems and view population, defense, and ore reserves.
